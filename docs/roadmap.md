# AllTheRooms Product Roadmap

## Stage 0 — Strategy and prototype (current)

- public destination content
- public host value proposition
- property-score acquisition page
- clickable seven-module host workspace
- shared Rincón sample property and market
- documented host and guest journeys
- no production authentication or persistent data

Exit criteria: product navigation, primary value proposition, and Rincón pilot workflow approved.

## Stage 1 — Property foundation

- authentication and team permissions
- manual property creation
- URL-assisted property draft
- property claim and verification
- source listing and booking-link management
- explainable preliminary score
- public property profile
- basic analytics and audit trail

Pilot target: 20 verified Rincón hosts and 40–60 properties.

## Stage 2 — Guest guide

- guide editor and templates
- public and protected information layers
- reservation or expiring guest access
- QR generation and printable templates
- recommendations library
- pre-arrival sharing
- engagement analytics
- English/Spanish content

Exit criteria: at least 60% of pilot stays open the guide and hosts maintain content.

## Stage 3 — Host community and services

- verified-market membership
- channels, posts, replies, reporting, and moderation
- provider onboarding and profiles
- service categories and service areas
- provider credential states
- host quote requests
- verified relationship reviews

Exit criteria: weekly useful community activity and provider response reliability.

## Stage 4 — Local businesses and offers

- local-business onboarding
- profiles, locations, and offers
- host inclusion and recommendation controls
- sponsored-label system
- guest views, actions, and redemption
- property and market eligibility
- business performance dashboard

Exit criteria: measurable guest usage without degrading host trust.

## Stage 5 — Market intelligence

- broader comparable-property datasets
- historical market observations
- confidence and source disclosures
- pricing opportunity alerts
- property improvement experiments
- multi-property portfolio views
- scheduled reports

## Stage 6 — Transactions and scale

Evaluate only after dense local engagement:

- direct booking or secure inquiry
- provider payments
- offer commerce
- subscription plans
- lead fees
- promoted provider placement
- local-business advertising
- additional destination launches

## Monetization hypotheses

1. Host premium intelligence and multi-property tools
2. Provider subscriptions or qualified leads
3. Clearly labeled local-business sponsorships
4. Offer transaction or redemption fees
5. Optional direct-booking and payment tools

The free property profile and basic guide should remain useful enough to sustain network growth.
