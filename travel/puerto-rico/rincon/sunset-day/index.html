In Rincón, sunset is not the finale. It is the main event.

A deliberately unhurried day built around coffee, salt water, local flavor, Punta Higüero Lighthouse, and the west coast’s daily sky show.

By AllTheRooms Editorial · Updated August 4, 2026
Best ruleArrive before golden hour
Classic viewpointPunta Higüero Lighthouse Park
Camera adviceStay after the sun drops

Rincón is nicknamed the town of beautiful sunsets, but the phrase undersells what happens: strangers pause, surfers turn toward shore, and the horizon briefly becomes public property.

8:00 a.m. — earn nothing

Begin with coffee and a slow breakfast. You do not need to “earn” relaxation with a punishing itinerary. Walk through town or take a gentle beach stroll while the light is soft.

10:00 a.m. — choose water by mood and conditions

If the ocean is calm and your group is prepared, swim or join a guided water activity. If swell is running, watch surfers at María’s or Domes. Spectating is not a consolation prize in a town where the best riders make physics look negotiable.

1:00 p.m. — lunch should take as long as it takes

Find fresh seafood, mofongo, a bakery, or a food truck that smells like the correct decision. Ask what is good today. Drink water. Move into shade. Refuse to optimize lunch.

3:30 p.m. — reset

Return to your room, rinse off, and rest. Golden hour is better when nobody is sunburned, dehydrated, or silently furious about parking.

5:00 p.m. — choose the stage

Panoramic

Punta Higüero Lighthouse

An elevated park with broad water views and easy pairing with Domes Beach.

Feet in sand

María’s Beach

Watch the final surf sessions cross through warm evening light.

Town-side ease

Western beaches

Choose a safe, accessible shoreline with a clean horizon and simple exit.

Private ritual

Your terrace

Sometimes the best viewpoint is the one with no parking and your own cold drink.

How to photograph it without missing it

Clean the phone lens. Lower exposure slightly to preserve sky color. Include a person, palm, board, or shoreline for scale. Take a few frames, then put the device down.

Stay after the sun disappears. Clouds often hold color for another fifteen or twenty minutes, and the cooler air changes the whole mood.

Leave no sunset evidence behind.

Pack out cups, food, and decorations. Avoid dunes and vegetation, respect private property, and use only legal parking.

Afterglow

Let dinner follow naturally. Choose somewhere casual, listen for live music, or take food back to the room. The day does not need a dramatic ending. The sky already handled that.
